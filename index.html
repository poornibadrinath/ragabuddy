Catalogue sources and notation

Janya names and scales are adapted from Wikipedia contributors (revision history, CC BY-SA 4.0). The adapted Wikipedia data retain this license. Names, swara notation, octave markers and variants were normalized for playback and searching. The Hamir Kalyani entry includes the teaching form documented by Raga Surabhi.

Historical readings come from D. Pattammal and M. N. Dhandapani’s Raga Pravaham. The app uses the pitch variants of the source’s parent Melakarta and interprets the endpoint Sa in the upper octave. Each entry links to its source page and reference code. These readings cover straightforward, legible rows. Notation and parent assignments can vary across sources and traditions.
